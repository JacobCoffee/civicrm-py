About Sample Data

The sample data generator creates realistic-looking data for demonstration:

  • Contacts: Random names, emails, and phone numbers. 70% individuals, 20% organizations, 10% households.
  • Activities: Various activity types linked to random contacts with dates in the past 30 days.
  • Contributions: Donations with amounts between $25 and $500, linked to random contacts.
  • Events: Future-dated events with various types (conferences, workshops, etc.).

Note: Activities and contributions require existing contacts. If you don't have any contacts yet, generate some contacts first.
